# Release fragment: Baden-Württemberg 1952

Added the 9 March 1952 election to the Verfassunggebende Landesversammlung to
`state_unharm`: 1,111 municipalities on the source's retrospective 1 January 1979
boundaries. The dataset now has 150,463 rows and 205 elections. The ten source
party counts are retained, including new `dg_bhe` and `uwg` columns. Existing
election values and all harmonized files are unchanged.

Municipal `eligible_voters` and `turnout` are NA because the source electorate
excludes 49,310 Wahlschein holders whose municipal distribution is unavailable.
The restricted source electorate and source turnout are preserved separately.
Voters and valid/invalid totals match the independent official totals. Two
one-vote source differences (KPD +1, SRP −1) remain explicitly documented.

CSV, RDS and Excel are regenerated. See the [source and validation note](../sources/bw_1952.md)
and `data/state_elections/derived/bw_1952/coverage.csv` for the coverage-manifest,
codebook and website integration fields. No historical crosswalk or harmonized
temporal coverage is extended.
