# Brandenburg 1990 and 1994 repair

The municipal second-vote results now use official polling-station spreadsheets instead of OCR. This note compares the replacement municipal counts with those spreadsheets, the original 1990 report, and statewide election totals. The repair preserves election-day geography and in-person voting coverage, as approved on 28 September 2026. Publication is pending review.

## Sources and geography

The statistical office publishes [1990](https://wahlergebnisse.brandenburg.de/wahlen/publikationen/dowmies/DL_BB_LT1990.xlsx) and [1994](https://wahlergebnisse.brandenburg.de/wahlen/publikationen/dowmies/DL_BB_LT1994.xlsx) workbooks. The tracked copies are `BB_1990_Landtagswahl_Wahlkreis.xlsx` and `BB_1994_Landtagswahl_Wahlkreis.xlsx` in `data/state_elections/raw/Landtagswahlen_Wahlkreis/Brandenburg/`. Despite this directory name, the relevant sheets contain polling stations. Their SHA-256 hashes are checked by `code/checks/check_bb_1990_1994.R`.

The 1990 workbook reflects state-border transfers effective in 1992. Its municipality identifiers also reflect later municipal changes; they cannot be used directly as election-day identifiers. We exclude 35 precincts in 22 municipality groups that originally voted in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern, map the remaining names to historical eight-digit official municipality identifiers (AGS), and restore Brunow and Dambeck from the original report. Those two municipalities account for the remaining 31 valid votes in the statewide reconciliation. The geographic reference is therefore reconstructed election-day geography, rather than an assumed common date for the workbook identifiers. The [geography evidence](../../data/state_elections/derived/bb_1990_1994/bb1990_geography_evidence.md) records the crosswalk, original report pages, ambiguous names, and manual checks.

For 1994, the first eight digits of the spreadsheet identifier give the municipal AGS, except that the four independent cities use their county prefix followed by `000`. All 1,693 resulting identifiers match the BBSR 1994 municipality list. The spreadsheet's final two identifier digits describe the Amt and are not part of the municipal AGS. No later-boundary substitution is required for this election.

## Coverage and reconciliation

Every party reconciles exactly after adding separately audited postal votes to in-person votes. Published municipal records contain in-person votes only. No counts are rescaled, and no pooled postal votes are allocated. `turnout` remains in-person voters divided by eligible voters; it does not measure total election turnout.

| Measure | 1990 | 1994 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Municipalities before repair | 1,793 | 1,644 |
| Municipalities after repair | 1,792 | 1,693 |
| Eligible voters | 1,955,403 | 1,933,680 |
| In-person voters | 1,264,341 | 1,018,160 |
| In-person valid votes | 1,226,539 | 1,001,516 |
| In-person invalid votes | 37,802 | 16,644 |
| Postal voters, excluded from municipal output | 47,779 | 71,001 |
| Postal valid votes | 47,367 | 70,503 |
| Postal invalid votes | 412 | 498 |
| All valid votes | 1,273,906 | 1,072,019 |

The 1990 decrease removes two false OCR identifiers for partial city precinct records and restores Zühlsdorf. Their precinct votes remain in the correct city totals. The 1994 increase restores 49 municipalities. Neither repair reduces municipality coverage by more than 20%. Repaired identifiers and voting totals have no missing values, and municipality–election keys are unique.

The existing party grouping is preserved. Domowina, whose 1,110 in-person votes were lost in the OCR-based output, is restored as a named column; unrelated elections have missing values in this new column. For 1994, `other` contains BürgerBündnis, DSU, GRAUE, ÖDP, and UWVB; the detailed count audit retains each separately. `freie_wahler` represents BFWG. The [party reconciliation](../../data/data_checks/bb_1990_1994/party_reconciliation.tsv) checks all 13 parties in 1990 and all 12 in 1994, including postal counts.

## Downstream outputs and checks

The baseline is commit `196e93a062c9166b0838382ce5a722f8737f878a`. Its `state_unharm.rds` SHA-256 is `8df5b7662a8a0883b7061207c76c3c2eb34080280e172ad0dba0822763314ff8`. All 147,075 unrelated source rows retain their values and types. The existing harmonizers rebuild both election years across all states because historical Brandenburg municipalities can contribute to destinations now in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern. Other election years are preserved exactly.

The independent scope audit found no changed recipient outside Brandenburg source lineage. The only changed destination in Mecklenburg-Vorpommern is `13076021` in 1990. The 2021 harmonization gains three 1994 records; the 2023 and 2025 harmonizations each gain two. None loses records. CSV/RDS round trips pass, and all five Excel exports pass hash, structure, and value checks. Every cell in all 14,027 Brandenburg/Mecklenburg-Vorpommern 1990/1994 export rows was compared with its CSV, including blanks, dates, and percentage formats. Metadata changes are confined to the repaired elections and the restored Domowina column.

The deprecated `state_harm` retains its historical method of population-weighted municipal shares. Reproducing all 826 affected baseline recipient rows required the Brandenburg subset of source commit `cf9abee`, retained with provenance checks in `data/state_elections/derived/bb_1990_1994/legacy_bb_source_cf9abee.rds`. The corrected legacy output has 829 recipient rows. Its direct historical crosswalk still leaves 19 source municipalities unmatched in 1990 (previously 21); these are listed in `legacy_unmatched_after.csv`. There are no unmatched 1994 municipalities. All 68,841 unrelated legacy rows, including 54 missing-AGS aggregates, are preserved. Use the maintained 2021, 2023, or 2025 harmonizations for current work.

The full `bundeslaendeR` comparison has no remaining Brandenburg 1990/1994 difference of one percentage point or more. Smaller differences reflect in-person versus all-vote denominators. One external party mapping is incorrect: the package maps GERDA's 1994 BFWG column to BürgerBündnis, producing a 0.741 percentage-point difference. These are distinct parties; direct source reconciliation verifies both. The remaining full-dataset deviation above one point is Rheinland-Pfalz 2006 (`linke_pds`, 2.557 points), outside this repair. Issue #7 should remain open for that follow-up rather than being declared fully resolved.

Rebuild commands, run from the repository root after hydrating the tracked source and crosswalk files:

```sh
Rscript --vanilla code/checks/rebuild_bb_1990_1994.R BASELINE_FINAL_DIR DEPENDENCY_ROOT STAGING_DIR
Rscript --vanilla code/checks/rebuild_bb_legacy.R BASELINE_FINAL_DIR/state_harm.rds LEGACY_OUTPUT_DIR
# Copy the validated legacy CSV/RDS from LEGACY_OUTPUT_DIR into the final directory.
Rscript --vanilla code/checks/check_bb_1990_1994.R
Rscript --vanilla code/checks/build_state_metadata.R
python3 code/export_excel.py --only state_unharm state_harm_21 state_harm_23 state_harm_25 state_harm
python3 code/checks/check_bb_excel.py
```

`BASELINE_FINAL_DIR` contains the five published state-election RDS files at the baseline commit. `DEPENDENCY_ROOT` contains hydrated crosswalk and municipal-covariate files at their repository-relative paths. Staging and legacy output directories must be separate from published outputs. Detailed counts, scope checks, source comparisons, and export verification are retained in `data/data_checks/bb_1990_1994/`.

## Prepared progress reply for issue #7

Thanks for flagging these discrepancies. We have prepared a repair for Brandenburg 1990 and 1994 using the official polling-station spreadsheets. It preserves election-day municipalities and the existing exclusion of pooled postal votes. All party counts reconcile exactly with official totals when postal votes are checked separately. The repair contains 1,792 municipalities in 1990 and 1,693 in 1994, with corresponding harmonized and Excel exports. The remaining small differences against statewide shares reflect postal-vote coverage and one party-name mapping in the comparison package. We are reviewing the outputs before publication. The separate Rheinland-Pfalz 2006 comparison still needs investigation, so we are keeping this issue open.
