# Baden-Württemberg constituent-assembly election, 9 March 1952

The addition contains **1,111 municipal records** for the *Wahl zur
Verfassunggebenden Landesversammlung* of 9 March 1952. It enters `state_unharm`
only. Party shares use valid single votes. This is an ingestion of the supplied
municipality source, with the source differences below retained, not a correction
of the official election result.

## Sources and geographic vintage

- `data/state_elections/raw/Landtagswahlen/Baden-Württemberg/2011 & früher/Gemeindetabelle_LW1952.xlsx`,
  sheet `Tabelle1`. Directory names may use decomposed Unicode; the importer
  discovers the exact basename recursively. The original file is read only.
- The accompanying `README BW LTW vor 2011.docx` records receipt from the
  Statistisches Landesamt and quotes its explanation of the geographic basis.
  The quoted correspondence explicitly assigns the 1952–2006 results to
  **1 January 1979 (1,111 municipalities)** and states that earlier results were
  converted following the municipal reforms. The README's heading says
  “2016 &2021”, but its quoted explanation expressly includes 1952. Its historic
  [delivery link](https://cloud.landbw.de/index.php/s/56MFtskMZHb2eK6) could not be
  retrieved during this audit; the held workbook and accompanying note are the
  provenance evidence.
- Independent totals: Statistisches Landesamt Baden-Württemberg,
  [*Vergleichs- und Strukturdaten zur Landtagswahl am 14. März 2021*](https://www.statistik-bw.de/fileadmin/user_upload/Service/Veroeff/Statistische_Berichte/423520001.pdf),
  B VII S – 2020, article 4235 20001, 15 October 2020, Table 1, printed pages
  4, 6, 8 and 10 (party-identity footnote continues on page 11). The 1952 column and the KPD/SRP discrepancy were verified
  against the PDF, including visual inspection of page 8, on 23 September 2026.

The six-digit source keys omit the state prefix. Prefixing `08` yields eight-digit
AGS, for example `111000` → `08111000` for Stuttgart. Both key columns in the
workbook agree, all 1,111 keys are unique, and the set matches the compilation's
1956 municipal keys. These are retrospective identifiers and boundaries, not
1952 administrative units. No GERDA crosswalk, interpolation, or boundary
conversion is applied. The retained `geography_date` in the source-count and
coverage tables is `1979-01-01`.

## Electorate and turnout

The workbook's footnotes in rows 1119–1120 define its electorate as the number
on the *Wahlliste*, excluding 49,310 Wahlschein holders statewide, and its
percentage turnout as voters divided by that restricted count.

The municipal restricted electorates sum to **4,332,807**, exactly the workbook's
standalone total in C1116. Adding the excluded **49,310** gives the independently
published full electorate, **4,382,117**. The workbook's voters sum to the official
**2,789,872**. Their full-denominator turnout is **63.665%** (officially published
as 63.7%); using the restricted denominator would instead give **64.389%**.

`state_unharm` therefore sets `eligible_voters` and `turnout` to **NA for all
1,111 records**. The municipality-specific excluded counts are unavailable and
are not allocated proportionally. Voters, valid and invalid votes are retained.
The restricted electorate and the source's turnout remain available as
`eligible_voters_wahlliste_only` and `turnout_wahlliste_only` in the derived
source-count table. They must not be substituted for standard electorate or
comparable municipal turnout. Wahlschein holders are not labeled postal voters:
the accompanying source note dates Baden-Württemberg's introduction of postal
voting at state elections to 1964.

## Counts and known source differences

Every municipal row satisfies both single-vote accounting identities: valid plus
invalid votes equals voters, and the ten party counts sum to valid votes.
Source percentages reconcile to the corresponding counts. No total, blank,
header, or footnote row enters the output. The only workbook total is the
electorate cell C1116; the other controls below are independent official totals.

The two one-vote differences are preserved. No evidence identifies a municipality
to amend, so totals are not rescaled or forced to agree. The importer requires
these exact, documented differences and rejects additional deviations.

Party tokens are `cdu`, `spd`, `fdp`, `bhe`, `kpd`, `dg_bhe`, `srp`, `zentrum`,
`uwg`, and `dg`. DG-BHE remains separate from BHE and DG; BHE is not renamed to
GB/BHE. The official table's footnote identifies the 1952 Zentrum as the
**Badische Zentrumspartei**; the existing `zentrum` token is retained with this
election-specific identity documented here. UWG is kept under its source label,
without an inferred merger into a later Freie-Wähler party.

All 14 source count fields are complete. Observed zero counts stay zero. Other
parties are NA, as are the two new party columns (`dg_bhe`, `uwg`) in every
pre-existing election. `other` is zero because all valid votes are accounted for;
`cdu_csu` duplicates CDU and must not be included in a sum of individual parties.

## Pipeline, outputs, and checks

`code/state_elections/parse_bw_1952.R` is called by the existing raw-data pipeline
before the 1956–2021 loop. It fails on changed sheet structure, headers,
footnotes, row/key coverage, numeric completeness, accounting, source
percentages, or statewide reconciliation. Its derived outputs are:

- `data/state_elections/derived/bw_1952/source_counts.csv`: source row, original
  key, AGS, name, election date, geographic vintage, restricted electorate,
  voters and absolute vote counts, and restricted source turnout.
- `data/state_elections/derived/bw_1952/reconciliation.csv`: official controls,
  observed differences and exact accepted differences, with source URL/pages.
- `data/state_elections/derived/bw_1952/coverage.csv`: a focused coverage entry
  for integration into the general coverage manifest, including denominator and
  harmonization decisions.

Regenerate and check from the repository root, with hydrated read-only inputs:

```sh
Rscript --vanilla code/state_elections/01b_state_unharm_raw.R
Rscript --vanilla code/checks/check_bw_1952.R \
  --baseline-rds /path/to/prior/state_unharm.rds \
  --baseline-csv /path/to/prior/state_unharm.csv
python3 code/export_excel.py --only state_unharm
python3 code/checks/check_bw_1952_excel.py
```

For this isolated-worktree audit, the complete baseline and updated pipelines
ran in temporary workspaces with links to the committed read-only Git LFS inputs.
The baseline reproduced the published RDS exactly. The regression check compares
every pre-existing value and missingness pattern, checks each new municipal party
count by reversing its share conversion, and compares CSV and RDS throughout.
Only the unharmonized CSV/RDS/Excel files change. The historical addition is
excluded by the existing `election_year >= 1990` harmonization filters; their
inputs on existing columns and all existing harmonized exports remain unchanged.

The addition increases `state_unharm` from 149,352 to **150,463 rows**, from
204 to **205 elections** (204 distinct state-years), and from 365 to **367 columns**. BW gains
one election, for **18 elections, 1952–2026**, in this checkout. General codebook,
website and package integration belongs to the companion documentation task.

Validation artifacts in `data/data_checks/bw_1952/` record 402 successful R
checks, the complete Excel comparison for the 1,111 added records, and input/
output SHA-256 hashes. The final preservation check covers 655 pipeline input
files and all 12 existing harmonized CSV/RDS/Excel files. Git LFS pointer checkout
line endings are normalized only for that comparison; source bytes are not edited.
