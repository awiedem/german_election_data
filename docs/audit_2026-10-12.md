# GERDA data audit, 2026-10-12

This audit checked all final datasets for accuracy against official totals, internal consistency, labeling, and units. Eleven Sonnet auditors each covered one area. A separate Sonnet verifier re-ran every finding and tried to refute it. A manager agent removed duplicates, ranked the findings, and re-ran four of the high-severity ones. Reproduction scripts and per-area outputs are in `data/data_checks/audit_2026-10-12/`. All findings with their verifier verdicts are in `findings_with_verdicts.json` in that folder. Item numbers (H1, M4, ...) refer to the tables below.

## Fixes applied on 2026-10-12

1. **Greens merged with Die Grauen (H1, also M11).** Cause: the party normaliser in `code/state_elections/01b_state_unharm_raw.R` matched `^DIE GR`, which also caught "DIE GRAUEN". The pattern now requires "DIE GRÜNE" (or GRUENE). Rebuilding with `01b`, `02b`, `04` and `05` changed only `gruene` and `graue`, and only in four elections: Berlin 2021 and 2023, Hessen 1991, and Baden-Württemberg 1992. Statewide Greens shares now match official results: Berlin 2021 18.88%, Berlin 2023 18.39% (below the SPD, as officially), Hessen 1991 8.81%, BW 1992 9.45%. All other cells are identical to the previous files. Remaining: in Berlin 2021/2023, `graue` combines Die Grauen and the separate Graue Panther list, which the Wahlkreis pipeline already splits. The deprecated legacy `state_harm` was not rebuilt and still has the old Greens values for Hessen 1991 and BW 1992.
2. **`pop_density_ags` in `state_harm_25` 1000 times too small (H5).** Fixed upstream in `05_state_harm_25.R` (population is in thousands; density is now multiplied by 1000). Berlin 2016 is 4,009 persons per km2 again.
3. **Rhein-Pfalz-Kreis Landrat 2017 under the Südliche Weinstraße AGS (H9).** The error is in the RLP source spreadsheet, which gives key 337 for that row. Both `01_mayoral_unharm.R` and `01b_mayoral_candidates.R` now recode it to 07338000. The final files `landrat_unharm` (1 row) and `landrat_candidates` (3 rows) were patched directly rather than by re-running the mayoral pipeline; all other CSV lines are byte-identical.

These fixes were merged with origin/main as of 270d757 and the state data rebuilt from the merged code (`01b`, `02b`, `04`, `05`, and `rebuild_bb_legacy.R` for the legacy file). Compared with origin/main, the only changes are the Greens/Grauen correction, the Brandenburg 1990/1994 repair, and the one documented Mecklenburg-Vorpommern 1990 destination (13076021) that the repair affects. `check_state_missingness.R` passed.

The 2026-10 changes on origin/main also resolved H10 (Schleswig-Holstein Kreiswahl rows dropped), H11 (Thüringen 2024 kreisfreie Städte added), H12 (TH 2019 Linke and BB 2014 Greens populated), M16 (`election_date` complete in `municipal_harm`) and M28 (Niedersachsen European 2024 voters). All other items below remain open.

## Summary verdict

The identifying keys, AGS formats, election dates, CSV/RDS parity and national totals are sound in most datasets. The problems are mostly in four places. (1) Gaps in source coverage: missing postal votes in older years, a Brandenburg 1999 shortfall of about 27%, and missing municipalities or parties. (2) Columns whose meaning differs from the codebook: the `eligible_voters` inflation, the harm-file share denominator, and `pop_density_ags` in `state_harm_25`. (3) A few clear data errors: Berlin Greens, the Berlin 1991 county population, the Rhein-Pfalz-Kreis 2017 AGS, and the Thueringen 2019 and Brandenburg 2014 party columns. (4) Documentation drift, since row counts and column lists in the codebook are stale across many files. All 11 audit areas completed. The manager re-ran four of the high findings and all four reproduced; Brandenburg 1999 (H2) and the Schleswig-Holstein vote-unit break (H10) were re-checked separately and also reproduced.

The manager did not re-fetch official results for any benchmark. The benchmark gaps rest on the auditors' comparisons to Bundeswahlleiterin and bundeslaendeR figures.

## Low-severity labeling and documentation items

- **`state_name` language.** It mixes English (Bavaria, Hesse, Saxony, NRW) and German (Niedersachsen, Baden-Wuerttemberg) within and across datasets. The mayoral and landrat files use German names.
- **Codebook and README stale.** Row and column counts are wrong for about 21 of 34 stated dimensions. Examples: `municipal_harm_25` 73,273 vs 84,757; `county_elec_unharm` 71,041x499 vs 78,090x595; `mayor_panel_annual` 253,437 vs 281,446. The mayoral known-issues file and the state README have the same problem. `checks_state_harm_output.txt` is stale. Several columns are undocumented (`pop`, `joint_mailin`, `person_id_method`, `ags_2023`, `w_pop`, others).
- **`bundeslaendeR_deviations.csv`.** It is stale: BB 1990/94 and NRW 1970 now match. It omits Berlin 2021/23, SSW, and the totals gaps.
- **Dead or empty columns.** `ags_21` in `federal_muni_harm_21` is 100% NA. `federal_muni_harm_25` lacks `pop_cw`. 12 party-block columns in the county files are entirely NA. `flag_unsuccessful_naive_merge` is only 0 or NA in `federal_cty_harm` and the federal muni harm files. `flag_briefwahl_only` is constant 0 in `state_unharm`.
- **`federal_muni_unharm` name and ID gaps.**
  - `ags_name` is NA for 29,626 rows (19.5%).
  - `county` holds "Hamburg" or "Berlin" in 23 rows.
  - `pop` is persons, and NA or 0 in most years, while harm files use thousands.
- **`federal_muni_raw`.** Party columns are counts at ballot-district level, under the same names as shares elsewhere.
- **Federal flags and rounding.**
  - `flag_total_votes_incongruent` fires in 29-38% of harm rows, mostly from rounding.
  - `flag_other_party_residual` fires on 25% of state harm rows and mainly signals a normal `other`.

- **Federal Wahlkreis and county files.**

- **Over-allocation of mail-in voters.** 92 federal rows have `number_voters` above `eligible_voters_orig` (max 3.30x). Flags exist but magnitudes are not documented.

  - `ags_name` is NA for large shares of some `county_elec_unharm` states.
  - Hessen has numeric names.
  - `ags_name` varies within an AGS for 214 mayoral AGS and 12 landrat AGS.
  - `ags_name` is NA for 9 Sachsen landrat rows in 2022.

## Refuted findings

- **NRW 2009 has no runoff rows (97 first rounds below 50%).** Not a data gap. NRW abolished the Stichwahl for 2009 and reinstated it in 2011, so the plurality winner is correct. Only documentation is missing.
- **BW 2026 vote type undocumented.** `data_pipeline.md` states that GERDA records the Zweitstimme. The open parser warning is already in TODO.md.
- **1990 federal harm totals shifted in BB/MV/TH.** The differences net to zero nationally and come from municipalities that changed state. This is expected behavior.
- **European harm file has 16 AGS present only in 2024.** Not reproduced. The set is empty; only older codes disappear (about 1.4% of units).
- **Harm rounding breaks identities in 61 rows.** The real count is 1,100-1,500 rows per year, with a maximum difference of 9 votes. Impact is trivial.
- **Crosswalk population and area are "of the source unit" per the codebook.** Not verified; left as unconfirmed.
