# Municipal supplement collected 27–28 October 2026

`source_rounds.csv` records 75 Hessen and 712 Baden-Württemberg rounds recovered from municipal ekom21/Komm.ONE APIs, legacy result pages, municipal notices and archived municipal websites. `source_candidates_public.csv` is the durable, numeric extraction, including aggregate/write-in and explicit Nein rows. Source URLs, checks and opaque runoff links are retained. `source_manifest.csv` records retrieval hashes; the original research cache and handoff are retained locally under `review/votemanager_2026-10-27/` and the external backup.

Run `python3 code/mayoral_elections/00_votemanager_supplement.py build` before the Hessen history parser and the R pipeline. This is offline and does not require the original browser session. The optional ignored `source_candidates_restricted.csv` supplies withheld names; it cannot change any numeric field, winner flag or runoff link. The one-time `import-review` command reconstructs the extraction from the research handoff and JSON cache (see the script's command help).

All 75 Hessen rounds pass accounting checks. They add 60 previously absent rounds and replace 15 hessenschau percentage-only rounds. They cover the 48 municipalities whose more recent results were never transmitted to HSL.

Of 712 BW tables, 710 pass accounting: Fischerbach 13 October 2024 reports only one of two districts; Schwäbisch Hall 4 July 2021 has a ten-vote accounting discrepancy. Both stay in the source inventory and metadata flags but are excluded from the augmented candidate input. Eschbach 7 May 2023 is a valid first-round table without a recovered decisive round; the pipeline retains the StaLA cycle. In 16 passing rounds the municipal counts disagree with StaLA (`metadata/votemanager_stala_conflicts_{html,json,additional}.csv`). StaLA takes precedence unless the Gemeinde's final notice confirms the municipal table (user decision 2026-10-29). Notices were found for 7 (Leibertingen, Bartholomä, Bad Liebenzell, Löffingen, Weingarten, Durmersheim first round and Neuwahl). Küssaberg 2021 is an exception without a notice: StaLA equals the municipal table minus postal district "Briefwahl 2" (626 voters, 4 invalid, 622 valid, 445 winner votes) on all four fields, so StaLA omitted that district. The other 8 keep StaLA. `metadata/votemanager_stala_conflict_decisions.csv` records each decision with its evidence. A decisive round on StaLA takes its whole cycle with it (Bösingen's first-round table is therefore unused); Sandhausen's StaLA first round is linked to its recovered runoff like Schorndorf's. Thus 702 tables enter, 700 of them in complete cycles. Missing first-round tables retain StaLA even when the runoff is recovered.

Schorndorf 7 November 2021 is separately flagged and not part of the 787 full-table inventory: the provisional municipal candidate table sums to 13,571 while StaLA records 13,570 valid votes. Its first-round fallback remains. The recovered runoff is used. No residual vote is assigned to a candidate.

Denkingen 18 June 2023 is corrected to Fabian Biselli 887 votes (StaLA typo: 878), with 414 votes for the other candidate and 1,301 valid votes. The final notice is in the archived municipal bulletin KW25/2023, linked in `source_rounds.csv`. Leonberg's 2017 elected person appears as Martin Kaufmann in the original result and Martin Cohn in the later register; the canonical published identity remains Cohn, supported by the municipal name-change notice recorded in the research handoff.

Newly collected non-winners' names are omitted from public inputs and outputs. Previously published candidate names and elected persons' names remain public, but a losing candidate's name stays public only if it was public in every round of theirs (user decision 2026-10-29: Dettenheim 2023's runoff row is now withheld too, and the older scrape outputs `raw/baden_wuerttemberg/bw_komm_one_parsed.csv` and `raw/hessen/he2026_parsed.csv` blank it and the Lauterbach (Hessen) 2026 candidate through `metadata/withheld_loser_rows.csv`); opaque pairing keys preserve the same votes and runoff joins without restricted names. `code/checks/check_votemanager_supplement.py` pins the source accounting, exclusions, same-year elections, Denkingen correction and final count coverage.


`metadata/votemanager_cycle_exclusions.csv` records two cycles that seated nobody (Obersontheim March 2021, annulled; Schwanau May 2022, office declined). Their election counts remain but the tenure panel excludes them. `metadata/votemanager_person_id_date_corrections.csv` preserves published IDs at twelve corrected cycle dates: six from recovered first rounds and six from corrected source dates. Three generic write-in totals remain aggregates, not individual candidates.


Ten StaLA dates and two round labels (one also misdated) are corrected from matching municipal tables via `metadata/bw_municipal_date_corrections.csv`: Holzgerlingen, Erligheim, Vaihingen an der Enz, Neudenau, Wallhausen, Laupheim, Amtzell, Boxberg and Singen (Hohentwiel). All four turnout/count fields must match before a key is changed. This removes duplicate representations of the same election; thirteen published cycle dates in total have explicit ID migrations. `01_mayoral_unharm.R` stops if one Gemeinde still shows the same four counts on two dates.

After correcting duplicate source dates, the combined election-level output gains 79 rounds: 60 HE and 19 BW. Fifteen existing HE percentage-only rounds receive full counts. The Bösingen runoff is dated 06.11.2022 despite the municipal title repeating 16.10.2022; its 1,870 valid / 17 invalid votes differ from StaLA's 1,871 / 16. No final notice was found, so StaLA's figures are used and the municipal ones stay in metadata.
